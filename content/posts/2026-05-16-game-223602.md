Microsoft is making waves in the gaming community with the rumored release of two new Xbox controllers: the Elite Series 3 and a dedicated Cloud Gaming controller. The latter is particularly intriguing as it appears to be designed for a new era of gaming, where cloud streaming is becoming increasingly popular. The Cloud Gaming controller features a compact design reminiscent of Joy-Cons, making it a potential game-changer for players who prefer a more portable setup.

The Elite Series 3, on the other hand, continues Microsoft's legacy of premium controllers, albeit with some fresh features like silver scrolling wheels that may allow for quick profile switching. With a 500mAh battery and support for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi 6, this controller promises to deliver a seamless gaming experience. As cloud gaming continues to evolve, these new peripherals could redefine how gamers interact with their favorite titles.

As we await official announcements from Microsoft, one has to wonder: will these new controllers set a new standard for gaming peripherals? The future of gaming is certainly looking exciting!
