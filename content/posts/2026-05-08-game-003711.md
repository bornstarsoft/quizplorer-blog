The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, an innovative puzzle game that challenges players to engage in lateral thinking and uncover hidden connections. This daily brainteaser presents a grid of 16 words or phrases, where players must identify the three related tiles in each row while spotting the imposter that doesnt belong. The thrill lies in the clever connections that bind the imposters together, making each puzzle a unique challenge that stimulates the mind.

Every day at midnight, a new Relink puzzle is released, encouraging players to return and maintain their streaks. With only four lives to use wisely, the stakes are high, and the satisfaction of solving the puzzle is immense. Relink not only tests your cognitive skills but also offers a fun way to engage with friends as you compare results and see who can crack the code first.

As puzzle games continue to evolve, Relink stands out by combining traditional gameplay with modern twists. Will you take on the challenge and see how quickly you can solve the next puzzle?
