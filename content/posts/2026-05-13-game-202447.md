The soundtrack for "Spirit Of The North 2" is set to captivate listeners with its ethereal soundscapes and previously unheard tracks, launching on streaming platforms next week. This update not only celebrates the games one-year anniversary but also introduces ten new tracks that enhance the immersive experience of the indie adventure puzzle game.

Composed by Pav Gekko, the soundtrack draws heavy inspiration from renowned Icelandic artists like lafur Arnalds and Sigur Rs. With a total of 93 tracks, including the new additions, the music aims to reflect the vastness and mystery of the Icelandic-inspired landscapes, moving away from traditional cinematic scores to create a distinct sonic identity. Gekkos use of ancient vocal techniques and ambient textures adds a layer of depth that resonates with the games themes of exploration and solitude.
